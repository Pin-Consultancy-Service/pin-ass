## 🚨 Red Flags to Avoid

❌ **Don't Buy Links** - Google will penalize you
❌ **Don't Keyword Stuff** - Looks spammy
❌ **Don't Copy Content** - Duplicate content penalty
❌ **Don't Ignore Mobile** - 60%+ traffic is mobile
❌ **Don't Skip Analytics** - Can't improve what you don't measure
